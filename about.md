# About

I am Hyungkyu (Tom) Lee, a Mechanical Engineering student at The Cooper Union.

## How I approach engineering

I am interested in understanding physical systems through experiments, modeling, sensing, and data. My main interests include building energy, thermal systems, HVAC, controls, and machine learning for physical systems.

My building energy work has evolved from whole-building analysis toward the operational data and measurements needed to understand individual equipment. That progression connects my interests in experimental characterization, energy optimization, and sensing.

## Beyond the research project

My independent work includes Space K, an LTE and LEO satellite communication concept developed during military service; Home Ally, a home maintenance marketplace concept with HVAC as its initial market; and Nudge, a product development and prototyping project.

## Graduate research direction

I want to pursue graduate research that connects physical understanding with useful measurements and data-driven methods, particularly in building energy, thermal systems, and controls.
