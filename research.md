# Research

Understanding energy and physical systems through experiments, models, operational data, and sensing.

## Building energy as a connected research question

My work at The Cooper Union initially focused on whole-building energy modeling. As the project progressed, I found that a larger limitation was the lack of reliable equipment-level operational data. This led me toward building monitoring, HVAC system characterization, and data-driven methods for understanding energy use at the equipment level.

[Read the building energy research overview →](projects/building-energy.md)

## Areas I want to investigate

- **Building energy and HVAC:** understanding how buildings and their equipment consume energy, and where opportunities for optimization may exist.
- **Thermal systems and controls:** connecting physical models, experimental characterization, and system operation.
- **Sensing and operational data:** obtaining reliable measurements at the resolution needed to understand equipment behavior.
- **Machine learning for physical systems:** exploring data-driven methods alongside physical understanding, including fault and anomaly detection.
